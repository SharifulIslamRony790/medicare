User Guide

How to use MediCare

Booking Appointments

Navigate to the Appointments section and click "Book Appointment". Select a doctor, choose an available date, and pick a time slot to confirm your booking.

Viewing Prescriptions

You can view all your prescriptions under the "Prescriptions" tab. Click on the print icon to download a PDF or print a physical copy for your records.

Billing & Payments

In the Billing section, you can see all your invoices. Click "Pay" on any pending invoice to securely complete your payment online using a card or mobile banking.
